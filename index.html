Everything a serious golf practice session needs

Built to be used mid-session, not after the fact

No end-of-round data entry. Log the shot, see the number, move to the next one — whether that’s a Distance Control session at the range, a short game session on the course, or a putting drill on the practice green.

Why this app

Built for golf practice, not another on-course tracker

Plenty of apps will track your on-course rounds shot by shot — GPS, club tagging, the lot. That's brilliant for some golfers. But we know it's too much for most: too much setup, too much tapping mid-round, too much data to make sense of afterwards.

No round-by-round setup

Nothing to tag mid-round. Practice sessions take seconds to start.

Built where you improve fastest

The range and the practice green — where the reps that actually move your numbers happen.

Simple by design

A handful of numbers that actually matter, not a stats page you need a manual to read.
