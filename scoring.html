How scoring works

Scored like a round. Fair at every level.

Two scores on every session. Strokes Gained tells you where you win and lose shots. Par scoring makes it feel like golf.

Strokes Gained

Every shot measured against a fixed baseline, so a putt, a chip and a drive sit on the same scale. It powers every trend in the app.

Par scoring

Finish close and it's a birdie. Miss by a mile and it's a double. Every shot rolls into a running score to par, just like a card.

Short game

A bunker isn't a fairway.
