FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a launch monitor to use the app?+

No. Distance Control works great with a rangefinder or by pacing off shots, and there's a self-rated 1–5 mode for sessions where you can't measure exact carry — you'll still see useful trends, just without strokes-gained on those sessions.

What is "strokes gained" and why does it matter?+

Strokes gained compares your result to a fixed baseline (from 30-handicap to PGA Tour) rather than just tracking raw distance or make percentage. It's the same method tour statisticians use, and it lets you compare a putt, a wedge shot and a drive on the same scale. See exactly how scoring works →

When is the app launching, and on which platforms?+

The Practice App is in final testing ahead of launch on iOS and as a web app, with Android to follow. Join the waitlist and you'll get an email the moment it's available to download.

Can I use it for Compete mode with friends who don't have accounts?+

Yes — Compete mode supports 2–4 players on one device for a head-to-head round. Only the host needs an account; the others just take their turns.

How does connecting with a coach work?+

From Settings, search for your coach and send a connection request. Once they approve it, your practice data — sessions, strokes gained, trends — is visible on their Coach dashboard. You can withdraw the connection at any time.

Is my data private?+

Your practice data is tied to your account and only visible to a coach you've explicitly connected with. You can export or delete your data from Settings at any time.

Does it work in metric or imperial units?+

Both — switch between yards/feet and metres in Settings at any time. Past sessions keep the units they were logged in for consistency.
