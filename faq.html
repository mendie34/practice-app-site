FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a launch monitor?

No. A rangefinder or pacing it off works fine. There's also a self-rated 1 to 5 mode for sessions where you can't measure carry.

What is Strokes Gained?

It compares every shot to a fixed baseline, from 30 handicap to PGA Tour, so a putt, a chip and a drive can be measured on the same scale. It's the method tour statisticians use. See how scoring works.

When does it launch, and where?

It's in final testing ahead of launch on iPhone and the web, with Android to follow. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the day it's live.

Can I compete with friends who don't have the app?

Yes. Compete mode runs 2 to 4 players on one phone. Only the host needs an account. Friends with accounts can also compare their stats with yours.

How does connecting with a coach work?

Search for your coach in Settings and send a request. Once they approve, they see your sessions and trends on their dashboard. You can disconnect at any time.

Is my data private?

Your data is only visible to you, a coach you've connected with, and friends you've approved. You can export or delete it from Settings at any time.

Yards or metres?

Both. Switch in Settings whenever you like. Past sessions keep the units they were logged in.
