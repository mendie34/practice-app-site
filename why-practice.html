The science bit

Why competitive & random golf practice actually work

Most golfers practice the way that feels comfortable, not the way that improves their game. These two ideas are built into the app by default.

Random beats blocked

Same club, same target, shot after shot feels productive — but you never hit the same shot twice on the course.

Changing the target every shot is harder in the moment, but it trains what actually transfers. That's why every drill defaults to randomized targets.

Pressure changes everything

A practice putt and a putt to win a tournament or get your handicap cut are physically identical, mentally worlds apart — and the course is the worst place to find that out for the first time.

Compete mode, or simply logging a score you can't take back, rehearses that pressure somewhere low-stakes, so it's familiar when it counts.
